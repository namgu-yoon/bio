About

I am a Research Assistant Professor in the Department of Biological Sciences at the Ulsan National Institute of Science and Technology (UNIST) and a Senior Researcher on the R&D team at Smartinbio. My research focuses on the mitochondrial chaperone TRAP1, from discovering and optimizing TRAP1 inhibitors to understanding how TRAP1 shapes cancer and the tumor microenvironment.

My work includes showing that mitoquinone inactivates TRAP1 by blocking its client-binding site J6, building a fluorescence-probe screening platform for allosteric TRAP1 inhibitors J10, and showing that TRAP1 is required for the pro-tumorigenic functions of cancer-associated adipocytes J12. I lead a Sejong Science Fellowship project on optimizing TRAP1 allosteric inhibitors as anticancer drugs (2022–2027).
